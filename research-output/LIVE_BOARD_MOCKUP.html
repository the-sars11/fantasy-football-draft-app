Reading the flags

FRAGILEInjury-prone, but the price already reflects it. Durability is baked into the range - not a reason to back off, just know the floor.
OUTUnavailable right now (IR, PUP, or ruled out). A real hit to this season - only a late stash, never a starter target.
hidden“Questionable” is not shown. 97 players wear it in camp - it is noise, so the board stays quiet unless the hit is real.

What changed, and why

1Strategy banner is gone. You said the pre-locked strategies all sit on top of each other. They do. This screen replaces the banner with a board that already reflects the winning move at this exact second.
2Live repricing off the room. As the auctioneer feed reports sales, the model re-reads dollars-still-in-the-room against dollars-still-needed. If RB is going for a tax, every RB target ticks up (the ▲) and every WR ticks down (the ▼). That is the “if the league overpays for RB, adjust” ask, built in.
3Repricing off your roster too. The You column is your target, not a generic one. A WR you still need gets a +$ your need bump the market does not see. Room is the field's price; You is yours.
4Value pockets surface themselves. Blue-railed rows are live pockets - worth more to you than the room is paying. The “+$5 pocket” chip is the gap, recomputed every pick, so you see the opening the instant it opens.
5A real dollar-bin plan. Star anyone during the draft and they hold at the top of the bin for when prices collapse. Under them, the model lists $1 to $3 darts that actually project to beat that price - not “whoever is highest left.”
6Two different injury truths, finally split. FRAGILE = injury-prone but priced in (Achane, McCaffrey) - keep targeting. OUT = hurt right now, real availability hit. “Questionable” is silenced entirely.
