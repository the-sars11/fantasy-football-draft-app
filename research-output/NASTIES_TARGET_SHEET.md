# THE NASTIES: DRAFT TARGET SHEET
**12-team · $200 · full-PPR · no-K · auction.** Roster: QB1 / RB1 / WR1 / TE1 / FLEX3 / DEF1 / Bench5.
Built from 26 strategies x 400 sims (seed 42) on **2026-08-26** data. Every price is a **walk-away** number: bid up to it, stop above it. Every figure on this sheet was re-verified line by line against `report.md` and `dataset.json` from this same pull.

> **How to read each number:**
> - **Worth** = the player's ceiling value in this league (ceilingValue).
> - **Room pays** = what the Nasties are projected to actually spend (expectedRoomPrice).
> - **Win at/under** = your disciplined target bid (the base of his value band). Land him at or below this and you won.
> - **Walk-away** = the hard ceiling (top of his value band). Never go a dollar over.
> - **The whole board is close.** Every strategy grades 8.4 to 9.0 wins. What wins your season is landing anchors at/under price and not overpaying, far more than which shape you pick.
