> **How to read each number:**
> - **Worth** = the player's ceiling value in this league (ceilingValue).

> - **Win at/under** = your disciplined target bid (the base of his value band). Land him at or below this and you won.

> - **The whole board is close.** Every strategy grades 8.3 to 9.0 wins. What wins your season is landing anchors at/under price and not overpaying, far more than which shape you pick.
