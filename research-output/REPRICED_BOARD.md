# Nasties Repriced Draft Board - Expert-Hedged

Prices driven by the **expert (ECR) rank** through your 16-yr Nasties room curve; worth set by **national-equivalent value** (room price with your positional inflation stripped out). Our old naive projection is shown as `OurModel` for context only - do NOT bid off it.

**Columns**
- `OurModel` = our optimistic projection worth. Context only; wildly high on year-2 breakouts (see Nix).
- `RoomPays` = what your room actually pays for a player at this ECR rank. **This is what you bid against.**
- `FairWorth` = what he's worth once your room's positional inflation is removed.
- `MaxBid` = your disciplined walk-away. Never pay above this.
- `Zone`: **VALUE** = your room underpays this position (worth > price -> hunt here). `prem` = your room overpays this position (still draftable, just cap at MaxBid, don't chase).
