The "What To Do" summary
  • HOLD · similar value still available, wait for the price to come to you
  • BID · go get him, here's your cap
  • PUSH · last of his tier, stretch above range if you want him
  • PASS · not your guy, or you marked him Avoid
  • One plain-English line under each, no jargon, no dashes, framed around your next move
What changed in v4
New
On-the-block "What To Do"
  • Target / avoid, your range, market estimate, and a directive move at a glance
  • Move verb (HOLD / BID / PUSH / PASS) + a cap + one plain line of why
  • Example: hold on Mahomes because Allen is similar value and still on the board
  • This is now the tallest, top thing on the screen. Your "what do I do now" answer
New
Fast block picker
  • "Change player" on the card, or a lost sync, opens a bottom sheet
  • Big search + one-tap shortlist of top available and your targets
  • Tap a name and they're on the block, pre-loaded with tier / range / tags
  • No typing needed in the common case. Just tap who the auctioneer called
Changed
Layout reorder
  • Tier context moved above the roster
  • My Team dropped to the bottom, compact two-column, glance only
  • Order now: on-the-block → what's next → budget → tiers → roster
  • Everything up top serves the now/next decision
Removed
Recent Sales — gone
  • Pulled off every screen. It did not help the now/next decision
  • Sale history still lives in Review if you ever want to look back
Build plan
FF-315: offline resync
  • Added to BUILD_PLAN.md as a spec item, not started
  • Auctioneer is the official system of record for players and draft
  • Come back online → reconcile your manual picks against auctioneer
  • Price mismatch → auctioneer wins, silent correction with a visible notice
  • Offline-only pick not yet in auctioneer → flagged "unconfirmed" until it appears
Kept
Research tab (unchanged ask)
  • Still the who-do-I-draft-next screen you bounce to in one tap
  • On-block mini-strip + inline record stays at the top there
  • Target View toggle + tappable tier chips stay
  • Recent Sales removed there too
